Problem

Who is in pain, how often, and what the pain costs them today.

Every student. Every month. And they don't even know it's happening.

  • 52% of Singapore adults don't know their monthly spend — worst in the 18–24 band (SmartWealth, 1,183 adults, Feb 2022)
  • Education isn't the gap — youths outscore adults on tested financial knowledge (NFCS via MAS, 2024). The habit is missing.
  • Today's tool is the Notes app, mental math, or nothing — until the money's already gone
MAS parliamentary reply Apr 2024 · SmartWealth survey Feb 2022
Solution

What does it do. Not what it enables. What it does.

It watches spending live and tells you the overspend before the month ends — with a number, a reason, and a fix.

  • Student logs a S$35 food-delivery expense → Guardian: "On track to exceed by ~S$65 — limit food to S$30 for the next 12 days"
  • Tap "Why am I seeing this?" → plain-language reason codes, no black box
  • Repeat small expenses get suppressed (cooldown); a real spike still breaks through and re-alerts
  • Cannot move money itself — recommendation only, student approves everything

Why now

What changed recently that makes this possible or necessary.

Three things lined up this year.

  • Agentic AI is now cheap and reliable enough to run per-transaction, deterministically guarded — ~S$0.005 per evaluation
  • Youth banking rails opened up: fee-free accounts from age 16 via Singpass (POSB, FRANK, CIMB) — the rails exist, nobody built the engagement layer on top
  • Singapore's SAFR framework (2026) gives agentic finance tools a clear regulatory frame to build inside, instead of a grey zone

Competition

How people solve this today, including the spreadsheet and the intern. Why yours wins for one specific person.

Everyone owns a piece. Nobody owns this student, right now.

  • The real competitor: the Notes app / mental math / nothing — most students track nothing at all
  • Seedly — trusted, bank-synced… built for salaried adults, and reactive (tells you after). S$3.98/mo
  • Bank apps — single-bank, generic views, no prediction
  • YNAB / Spendee — powerful, but not localized (no hawker / MRT / CCA categories) and too complex for a first budget
For a 17-year-old poly student with a S$600/month allowance and zero tracking habit, we're the only option that's proactive, free, and speaks their categories.
